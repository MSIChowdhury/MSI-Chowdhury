Research

I work on AI for autonomous navigation and exploration. My goal is to build robots that can map, reason about, and explore unfamiliar environments safely and efficiently, combining learning-based perception with planning so that they hold up under noisy, real-world conditions.

Previously, I studied how vision-language models can detect failures in robotic manipulation, benchmarking edge-deployable models such as Moondream2 and SmolVLM against larger models such as Qwen-2-VL 7B and Florence-2.

Selected Projects

  • IoT-enabled Scalable Closed Hydroponics System. An automated hydroponics system with pH, EC, CO2, humidity, and temperature monitoring, built on Arduino-based sensor nodes with a live web dashboard, XGBoost-based control decisions, and a persistent sensing error-resilience algorithm. Funded by an ICT Innovation Fund grant from Bangladesh's ICT Division, where I served as Co-PI. Experiments showed a 78–288% improvement in fresh mass yield over conventional techniques.
    • Selected publication: Design of an IoT-enabled Scalable Closed Hydroponics System. Under review.
  • 2-DOF Single Claw Crawler Bot using Q-learning. A two degree-of-freedom crawler robot that learns its own gait: Q-learning with epsilon-greedy exploration optimizes servo-angle sequences for maximum displacement.
